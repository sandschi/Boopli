The Boopli panel is available in English (en), German (de), Dutch (nl), French (fr) and Spanish (es). The panel follows Home Assistant’s language. Select another language for this browser in **Settings → Language**. The `?lang=xx` option in the address takes priority. If you use AI help or Ask Claude, the AI service you choose receives what you share and may keep it if it runs in the cloud. See [AI help](/Boopli/ai-help/) for details.
