title: Your pet
description: Pick a pet, its room and its moods.

Pick a pet in Settings (the setup wizard asks too): slime, bear, cat, dog or fox. The pet lives in a small room at the top of Today. It has its own mood (happy, content, sleepy, sad, hibernating) that shows in its face and how it moves, and things appear in the room as it levels up (a water bowl, a food bowl, a bed). While away mode is on, a little suitcase stands next to it. When it is sleepy or hibernating it sleeps in its bed with the blanket pulled over it. While you work on a step (**On it**) it does housework with you (it wipes the floor, washes a sock and takes out the trash), and it types on a laptop while the AI writes your steps. It also reacts when you finish a step, level up, or wake it up, and when you tap it. **Preview your pet** in Settings opens a window where you can play every mood, activity and moment on demand and see the room at every level. The slime is drawn and animated in code (nothing to download); the other pets show an emoji until their art is ready. Animations pause while the page is hidden and follow your system's "reduce motion" setting.

## Moods

The mood follows how long ago you last finished a step:

| Mood | When |
|---|---|
| Happy | You finished a step today. |
| Content | The last step was yesterday, or you have no chores yet. |
| Sleepy | 2 days without finishing a step. |
| Sad | 3 to 6 days. |
| Hibernating | 7 days or more. |

While [Away mode](/Boopli/away-mode/) is on, the mood stays as it was. The mood, the level and the XP are also available as [sensors](/Boopli/automations/).

## Accent colour

The **Your pet and accent colour** card in Settings also sets the colour of Boopli's buttons, bars and highlights. By default it is your pet's own colour. Pick one of the swatches, or type your own, if you prefer another. The Settings card shows the colour that is set, and **Save** applies it everywhere.
