title: Your pet
description: Pick a pet, its room and its moods.

Select a pet in Settings (the first setup wizard asks too): slime, bear, cat, dog or fox. The pet lives in a small room at the top of Today. It has its own mood (happy, content, sleepy, sad, hibernating) that shows in its face and how it moves, and its room shows what you bought for it in the shop (a water bowl, a food bowl, a bed, a color or pattern on the wall and the floor). Your pet can also change its fur color and wear a scarf. While away mode is on, a little suitcase stands next to it. When it is sleepy or hibernating it sleeps in its bed with the blanket pulled over it. While you work on a step (**On it**) it does housework with you (it wipes the floor, washes a sock and takes out the trash), and it types on a laptop while the AI writes your steps. It also reacts when you finish a step, level up, or wake it up, and when you interact with it. **▶ Preview your pet** in Settings opens a window where you can play every mood, activity and moment on demand and see its room. All five pets are drawn and animated in code (nothing to download). Animations pause while the page is hidden and follow your system's "reduce motion" setting.

## Moods

The mood follows how long ago you last finished a step:

| Mood | When |
|---|---|
| Happy | You finished a step today. |
| Content | The last step was yesterday, or you have no chores yet. |
| Sleepy | 2 days without finishing a step. |
| Sad | 3 to 6 days. |
| Hibernating | 7 days or more. |

While [Away mode](/Boopli/away-mode/) is on, the mood stays as it was. The mood, the level and the XP are also available as [sensors](/Boopli/automations/).

## Blubs, feeding and levels

Finishing steps earns **Blubs**, the pet's currency. A small step gives 10 Blubs, a medium one 15 and a big one 20. The step that finishes a chore gives 20 more, and a step done within 10 minutes of its turn gives 5 more. One chore earns Blubs on at most 3 runs a day, so running it over and over does not pay. Blubs belong to the whole household, and nothing ever takes them away except spending them.

XP and levels only come from **feeding**. Buy food in the shop, then feed it from the inventory:

| Food | Costs | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| Small | 10 Blubs | 5 XP |
| Medium | 25 Blubs | 15 XP |
| Big | 50 Blubs | 30 XP |

The pet eats up to 3 meals a day. After that it is full: it takes no more food and gives no more XP, and the food stays in your inventory for tomorrow. Today shows how many meals it ate. Nothing is ever taken away and the pet never nags about it. A few steps already pay for a meal, so the first level comes quickly. Levels get slower after level 10 and flatter after level 20, and there is no maximum level. At level 10 a short message says the starter quest is done.

A household that is new to Blubs, or that had a pet before Blubs existed, starts over with 30 Blubs once.

## The shop

Select the **Blubs** field at the top left of the pet's room to open the shop. It sells food (again and again), things for the room, colors and patterns for the wall and the floor, things for your pet to wear, and upgrades. A thing you bought is gone from the shop and waits in your inventory. A thing you cannot buy yet says why: from level 3, for example, or that it needs an achievement. The field turns green when something new is unlocked and you have enough Blubs for it. That is the only sign, nothing blinks. Choose an item in the shop to see what it does, then press **Buy** at the bottom. The button at the top takes you to the inventory.

- **Water bowl** (level 1), **food bowl** (level 2) and **bed** (level 3) appear in the room as soon as you buy them.
- **A color and a pattern** can be put on the wall or the floor.
- Under **For your pet**, you'll find a fur color (**Snow fur**) and a scarf (**Red scarf**) for your pet to wear.
- The **Bowl gem** sits on the food bowl. You need the bowl first and a higher level.
- The **Dog bone** is only for dogs.
- **A 4th and 5th meal a day** (from level 5 and level 10) raise the number of meals before the pet is full. They work for good, with no timer.

## The inventory

Select the button next to the Blubs field to open the inventory. Here you feed the pet from your food, and you choose what the wall and the floor look like: a color, a pattern, or plain. Under **What {name} wears**, choose a color or **Own color** for **Fur**, and a scarf or **Default** for **Neck**. With **Default**, your pet goes back to what it usually wears around its neck. A cat keeps its bow tie, for example. You can change these choices any time, and nothing is paid back. Choose a food, a color or a pattern, then press **Feed** or **Use** at the bottom. The inventory also lists the things that stand in the room. The button at the top takes you to the shop.

## Accent color

The **Your pet and accent color** card in Settings also sets the color of Boopli's buttons, bars and highlights. By default it is your pet's own color. Select one of the swatches, or type your own, if you prefer another. The Settings card shows the color that is set, and **Save and close** applies it everywhere.
