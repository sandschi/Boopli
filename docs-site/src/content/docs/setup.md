## First setup

The first time you open Boopli, a wizard opens by itself. A rail on the left lists the steps, with one question per screen, a progress bar, a Back button and a safe default everywhere. Every step can be skipped, and **Skip for now** closes the whole wizard. Answers are saved as you go, so closing the tab halfway loses nothing. It asks for: your pet's name, your phone, quiet hours, snooze length and default nudge style, reminder lights, whether you want speaker reminders (a single question: **Set up speakers** or **Skip**), where you are (a person entity, so Boopli knows when you are away), and a few starter chores (Dishwasher, Laundry, Trash, Pills, pre-ticked, added without a schedule so nothing nudges you until you set a time or start one). Nothing is sent to any device until you press a **Test** button.

If you skip or leave it unfinished, Today shows a **Finish setting up** banner that continues where you stopped. Installs that already had chores or settings before the wizard existed count as set up.

## Quiet hours are per day

Every weekday has its own times, or "no limit". Nothing is grouped into "weekdays" and "weekend", because not everyone has the same weekend. Shortcuts like "Same as Monday" only fill in the times.

## Two views, Simple and Advanced

Settings has two views, switched with a button at the top (Boopli remembers your choice in the browser):

- **Simple (the default).** One card per area (your pet, phone, quiet hours, snooze and style, reminder lights, speakers, alarm devices, where you are) saying in plain words what is set, with a **Set up step by step** button. Each area has its own wizard with a quick path and the optional details (blink length and repeats, when each channel joins in, alarm length). Speakers are set up in small steps: pick a speaker, a ding, a spoken sentence, an alarm sound, when each joins in; alarm devices have their own wizard.
- **Advanced.** All the forms, side by side, for people who prefer them.

Every wizard ends with **All set**, which lists exactly what changed compared with before that wizard (for example "Snooze: 15 to 10 min" or "Mon: 07:00 to 22:00 to 07:30 to 22:00").

## Unsaved changes are always visible

On the Settings page and in the chore editor, a bar sticks to the bottom of the screen as soon as you edit anything. On Settings it lists which cards have unsaved changes: press a name and the page scrolls to that card and lights it up. The changed cards carry an orange "Unsaved" tag, the Settings and Chores tabs show a dot, and leaving with unsaved edits (another tab, a wizard, closing the page) asks **Save / Discard / Stay**.

## Settings are one calm page

Cards in three groups: Devices (phone, lights, speakers, alarm devices), Timing (quiet hours, snooze and timing, Nudge it your way, where you are) and Extras (your pet, wall board, AI help). Each card shows what is set in a few lines and has two buttons: **Guide me** (a step-by-step guide) and **Advanced** (a window with every detail; the Wall board card has **Edit on the wall** instead, which opens the wall board in edit mode). The Advanced window has **Save and close**, **Discard and close** (throws away what you changed in that window) and **Keep for later** (closes it and keeps your changes as an unsaved draft).



## Start again

At the bottom of Settings, **Run the first setup again** opens the first-run wizard once more. Every wizard ends with the list of what changed, so you can see exactly what you did.
