Boopli turns chores into small steps and nudges you through them with the lights, speakers and phone you already have. Boopli runs in Home Assistant and needs no Boopli account. It sends nothing to an AI service by itself. If you use AI help or Ask Claude, the AI service you choose receives your chore, answers and any room notes or photos you share. A cloud provider may keep that data. See [AI help](/Boopli/ai-help/) for what gets shared.

These pages explain how to set it up and how to use each part of it. You do not have to read them in order. Use the menu on the left, or the search at the top.

The panel is available in English (en), German (de), Dutch (nl), French (fr) and Spanish (es). The panel follows Home Assistant’s language. Select another language for this browser in **Settings → Language**. The `?lang=xx` option in the address takes priority.

## If you are new

1. [Install Boopli](/Boopli/install/) with HACS or by hand.
2. [Set it up](/Boopli/setup/). A wizard asks one question per screen, and every step can be skipped.
3. Look at [the Today screen](/Boopli/today/). It is where you do things.
4. Make your first chore in [Chores and schedules](/Boopli/chores/).
