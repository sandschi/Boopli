Today is where you do things. It shows your pet in its room at the top, and under it what is due now.

![The Today screen: the step waiting for you with Done and On it, the pet in its room, and what is ready now](/Boopli/img/today.jpg)

<img src="/Boopli/img/today-phone.jpg" alt="The Today screen on a phone" width="260" />

The panel fits a phone too, so you can use it from the Home Assistant app.

## What is on it

- The current step shows its group, a progress bar and a count such as “Step 3 of 8.” A celebration shows when a chore is finished.
- **Done.** Marks the step done. The nudges for it stop.
- **On it.** Tells Boopli you are working on the step. It pauses nudges and can send a phone check-in after the check-in time. Select **Resume nudges** to take it back. See [Chores and schedules](/Boopli/chores/).
- **Snooze** on Today, your phone or the wall board delays the next nudge. The step stays open and Today shows when the snooze ends.
- **Ready now.** Other steps that are your turn right now, grouped by chore.
- **Still to do today.** The rest of what is going on today: the waiting steps of chores in progress, and chores due later today with their time. Select **Start now** on a step that’s waiting for a time or for something to happen.
- **Upcoming.** Scheduled chores on later days (the first eight, with the rest in the Chores tab).
- **Set aside for later.** Parts of a chore you put away, each with **Start now**, **Change time** and **Delete**.
- **Done by itself.** Steps that finished through a sensor, an NFC tag or a button, with what happened.
- **Away mode is on.** A big amber banner while [Away mode](/Boopli/away-mode/) is on.
- **Finish setting up.** A banner that continues the setup wizard where you stopped.

## Starting a chore

Write what you want to get done and select **Break it down now** to make a one-off chore that disappears when it is done, or **Break down a recurring chore** to make a chore with a schedule. If you’ve set up [Ask Claude](/Boopli/ask-claude/) and your Home Assistant AI agent is off, the button is **Ask Claude to break it down**. See [AI help](/Boopli/ai-help/).
