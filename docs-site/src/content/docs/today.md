title: The Today screen
description: What the Today tab shows and what its buttons do.
---

Today is where you do things. It shows your pet in its room at the top, and under it what is due now.

## What is on it

- **What to do now.** The step that is waiting for you, with its group, "Step 3 of 8" and a progress bar. A celebration shows when a task is finished.
- **Done.** Marks the step done. The nudges for it stop.
- **On it.** Tells Boopli you are working on the step. It stops nagging and asks once after the check-in time, "Still on it?". See [Chores and schedules](/Boopli/chores/).
- **Snooze.** Delays the next nudge. The step stays open.
- **Ready now.** Other steps that are your turn right now, grouped by chore.
- **Still to do today.** The rest of what is going on today: the waiting steps of chores in progress, and chores due later today with their time.
- **Upcoming.** Scheduled chores on later days (the first eight, with the rest in the Chores tab).
- **Set aside for later.** Parts of a task you put away, each with **Start now**, **Change time** and **Remove**.
- **Done by itself.** Steps that finished through a sensor, an NFC tag or a button, with what happened.
- **Away mode banner.** A big amber banner while [Away mode](/Boopli/away-mode/) is on.
- **Finish setting up.** A banner that continues the setup wizard where you stopped.

## Starting a task

Write what you want to get done and press **Break it down now** to make a one-off task that disappears when it is done, or **Break down a recurring chore** to make a chore with a schedule. With [Ask Claude](/Boopli/ask-claude/) on, there is also an **Ask Claude to break it down** button. See [AI help](/Boopli/ai-help/).
